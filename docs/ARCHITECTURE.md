# Architecture

Where things live and the rules that keep them working, moved from `CLAUDE.md`.

- `scripts/app.gd` (`scenes/app.tscn`, the project's main scene) - start menu, in-game menu, saving and loading; it runs `scenes/main.tscn` as the game. Tests create `main.gd` directly. Saves: `scripts/persistence/` (`GameState` decides what is saved; domain fields are saved automatically, but new object-valued fields, caches, and new `main.gd` state need a line there). Design: `docs/SAVED_GAMES.md`.
- `scripts/main.gd` - large central scene script: map input, drawing, UI, day ticking, trade. High-risk; avoid adding new simulation logic here. A click that switches views must call `get_viewport().set_input_as_handled()`, or the newly shown view receives the same click.
- `scripts/domain/` - simulation model: `city.gd` (resources, households, buildings, growth, lifecycle orchestration), `household.gd` (family structure, lifecycle, labor), `building.gd`.
- `scripts/simulation/` - `calendar.gd` and `simulation_clock.gd` (1 day = 1 real second at 1x).
- `scripts/world/` - regional and local map generation, founding-site helpers, and the world map's data (`world_map_data.gd`), generator (`world_generator.gd`, `world_name_generator.gd`), authored planet (`fixed_world_definition.gd`), and builder (`world_field_builder.gd`). The game plays on a planet generated from a new seed each run (`-- --world-seed=N` on the command line replays one); tests that look places up by name set `main.use_authored_world`. Generated worlds must stay deterministic per seed.
- `scripts/visuals/world_map/` and `shaders/world_terrain.gdshader` - the world atlas renderer; the game opens on it (`VIEW_WORLD` in `main.gd`). Design, decisions, and remaining integration work: `docs/WORLD_MAP.md`.
- `scripts/world/regional_terrain.gd` (+ `regional_chunk_generator.gd`), `scripts/visuals/region_map/`, and `shaders/region_terrain.gdshader` - the regional map: the whole planet at true scale (1 tile = 1.2 world units), generated in 60x60 chunks on worker threads; every tile must stay a pure function of its global position, or chunks stop joining. `main.gd` runs its rules on a 160x100 neighbourhood (`extract_window`), and `RegionMapView` streams the landscape under `main.gd`'s panels. The original `region_map_generator.gd` path must stay byte-identical (`tests/region_map_validation.gd` checks fingerprints). Decoration must never show what the tile data does not hold. Call `RegionalTerrain.wait_for_tasks()` before quitting a script that streamed chunks, or Godot hangs on exit. Details: `docs/REGIONAL_MAP.md`.
- `scripts/world/local_city_map_generator.gd`, `scripts/visuals/town_map/`, and `shaders/town_terrain.gdshader` - the town map: the regional land around the site magnified 4x (64x44 town tiles = 16x11 regional tiles), drawn in the regional map's style under a pan and zoom camera on its own canvas layer under `main.gd`'s panels. `main.gd` keeps the rules and routes town-map clicks through `TownMapView` (`map_clicked`); never draw the town in `main.gd`'s `_draw`. Town maps must stay deterministic per site (no game RNG). Painters shared with the regional map: `scripts/visuals/landscape_painter.gd`, `building_painter.gd`. Details: `docs/TOWN_MAP.md`.
- `scripts/ui/`, `scripts/visuals/` - panels, helpers, drawing. The town view's sidebar is `scripts/ui/town_sidebar/` (`TownSidebar` and its four pages; `main.gd` passes it the town, forwards the mouse, and carries out what a click asks in `apply_city_action`). Design: `docs/TOWN_UI.md`.
- Daily `City.tick()`, monthly `advance_household_lifecycle_month()`, and seasonal birth rolls are driven by calendar signals in `main.gd`.
- Performance limits and their causes are measured in `docs/HOUSEHOLD_SIM_BENCHMARK.md`.
- Every economy number (starting stock, outputs, upkeep, construction costs and work, travel) lives in `scripts/domain/economy_balance.gd`.
- Building goes through one pipeline for the player and for families: `TownConstruction` (`city.construction`: requests, queue, builders, completion), `TownLogistics` (`city.logistics`: stores, routes on the town map through tiles and along the lines between them, the share of a workplace's day lost to travel, trails and paths worn where families walk, and trampled and fenced fields), and `TownWoodland` (`city.woodland`: woods marked for clearing, woodcutters felling them (and the nearest trees, slowly, when nothing is marked), and woods spreading through each growing season onto open land nothing holds), `TownFarmland` (`city.farmland`: land marked to till, tilled into farmland by the fields' families), `TownErrands` (`city.errands`: families' trips from home to the store, water, woods, and kin, wearing paths), and `FamilyDevelopment` (families building homes on residential plots, weekly). Nothing is placed finished in play: `City.add_building` is what a finished project calls. Design and judgment calls: `docs/TOWN_BUILDING_MODEL.md`.
- `TownLand` (`city.land`) is what the land in reach gives and threatens: fishers' and hunters' catch, herders' grazing, the seasons' yield on fields, high water, wolves. Land in reach is shared: each water, woods or pasture tile is divided among the worked workplaces of its kind whose reach holds it (`count_share`), in real output and in what families expect, so no land is counted twice (metas `woods_not_shared`, `water_not_shared`, `pasture_not_shared` restore the old counting for comparison). Design and the milestone plan for the first stage: `docs/FIRST_STAGE_LOOP.md`.
- `FamilyAmbitions` (static, monthly from `HouseholdMigration.advance_month`) is what families want (a home, a fenced field, work for their calling) and their contentment, which raises the hardship they endure and draws newcomers. Families fence their own fields; under the classic rules they trade auto-assigned work toward their callings (player assignments are never touched).
- `OccupationReview` (static, monthly, after ambitions; families-lead towns): families reconsider their work, rarely, when their own circumstances change, and weigh staying against other work (`docs/OCCUPATION_RECONSIDERATION.md`). It replaces the calling swap where families lead. An essential shortage coming (`FamilyInitiative.get_shortage`) brings a review forward (`docs/AUTONOMOUS_OCCUPATIONS.md`).
- `FocusedAppeal` (static; state on `TownInfluence.appeals`, checked daily from `City.tick`): the gathering's temporary request for one good (toolmaking, food, wood), weighed in families' choices of work on that good only; separate from the Community Priority and the wisdoms (`docs/AUTONOMOUS_OCCUPATIONS.md`).
- Kept woods (`TownWoodland.protected`, tool `protect_woods`, key V): a binding designation; no woodcutter fells a kept tree, and woodcutters bring in less the farther the trees they may fell lie (`TownWoodland.get_felling_factor`, applied in `TownLand.get_output_factor`).
- The work families choose (`FamilyInitiative.get_occupations`) is every workplace with a hand-work stage in `BuildingTiers.TIER0_TO_TIER1`, weighed by the good it makes (`get_activity`) and the land in reach (`TownLand.get_reach_kind`): a new trade is a family's choice once it has a hand-work stage, with no decision code of its own. Comparison-only city metas (`occupations_first_three`, `no_shortage_recognition`, `shortage_missing_trade_only`, `occupations_left_out`) restore earlier behaviour or isolate a trade (`docs/AUTONOMOUS_OCCUPATIONS.md`).
- `HouseholdEconomy` (static; daily `plan_day` before production and `work_day` after it, `feed` from `City.consume_food`, monthly `advance_month` after the occupation review; off with `TownInfluence.self_sufficiency`): each family's secondary work (garden, wood, mending) out of its working day (`City.get_work_time_factor`), its food larder and its own short days (hardship and hungry days per family), and proficiency by trade (learning, decay, transfer, inheritance; `get_output_factor` scales a holding's output) (`docs/HOUSEHOLD_SELF_SUFFICIENCY.md`).
- `TownRegard` (static, monthly from `TownSocial.review_month`, weekly stance from `run_week`; state on `TownSocial` and `Household.noticed_merit`): families notice each other's new achievements and admire or resent them; influence in parts; from Village, the Speaker of the Gathering the Elder appoints, its lead on the priority, first claim, and mediation (`docs/EMERGENT_RECOGNITION_AND_LEADERSHIP.md`). It replaces the player's honours and the Reward helping toggle.
- `TownTrade` (`city.trade`) is traders from the neutral town: the town's link (a road from its heart to the map's edge, or a river near it), visits in summer and autumn, and barter lots priced in grain. The game names the partner after the nearest neutral town; the player takes lots from the trader's card under the sidebar's Needs attention (`SidebarOverviewPage.get_trader_card`).
- `TownLedger` (static; sprint S6) is who owns what in the one store: `city.resources` stays the only physical stock; families hold shares in it (`Household.stores_share`), the community's goods are the rest (computed, never stored); a family's own project is paid from its share first and the building records who paid (`Building.funded`); raids fall on every owner in proportion; the player trades only the community's goods. While every share is zero (pooled) it changes nothing.
- `TownCompact` (static; sprint S7) is the founding compact: `city.compact` holds the community's share of each category of work (empty: pooled, the game before S6). A holding's output is shared at the rate with the family working it; along a chain, inputs' worth goes back to their owners and only the added worth is shared (`deliver_output`). Under a compact families eat, warm, build and keep up their own holdings from their own goods first, keep their side work, and may give more monthly by their values and trust. Founding asks for a compact (`main.gd`'s founding panel); the Community page changes it.
- `TownCommons` (static; sprint S8) is how the community's goods answer under a compact. It covers:
  - the table, which feeds everyone from the community's food above the player's reserve (`feed_from_table`, called from `HouseholdEconomy.feed`);
  - a family still short, which eats its larder, then asks the households it knows, who give from their own by the share desire (`ask_for_food`), then sells for food;
  - only after all that, the reserve, and only for a family in danger. Own means come first and the reserve last (owner, 2026-11-07), and the draw is limited only by what the reserve holds;
  - monthly grants by the player's rule, judged for fairness.

  Pooled towns don't use it.
- `TownMarket` (static; sprint S9) is exchange between households under a compact: a monthly market day (`hold`, from `HouseholdMigration.advance_month`) where families barter what they hold beyond their needs, paid in food, at prices from the town's demand and supply, cleared in aggregate with nothing owed; Insular families bring less, Hagglers take more of what buyers pay. A hungry family may sell for food any day (`sell_for_food`, the last step of `TownCommons.feed_from_table`). Wealth (`get_wealth`) is a level that `TownSocial.is_prosperous` reads under a compact.
- `LandUseRecord` (`scripts/world/`; master roadmap Phase 1, `docs/phase1/P1-2_land_use.md`) is what a town has done to the land, per regional tile: its town tiles counted as built, farmland, street, cleared woods and wooded, against the land under it before founding. It is derived from the saved town map and the position-pure land (`LocalCityMapGenerator.generate_base_from_terrain`), so it is never saved. The continuous regional view holds the neighbourhood's record (`RegionMapView.land_use`, rebuilt with its monthly refresh) and keeps trees off woods a town has felled. Regional chunks stay a pure function of position, because town maps are generated from them.
- Geography that bites (Economic Foundations Track A, `docs/ECONOMIC_FOUNDATIONS_ROADMAP.md`):
  - `TownDeposits` (static): stone, clay and limestone deposits that quarries, pits and brickworks must stand on and work out, tile by tile (`deposit_left` on the tile).
  - `TownWoodland`: finite, regrowing woods that a woodcutter's timber comes out of.
  - `TownFuel` (static): winter firewood from the common store, by season, site and shelter.
  - `HouseholdMortality` (static): sustained severe hunger or cold can kill, with seeded rolls.
  - `MaterialChoice` (static): a family's choice of timber or stone for its own buildings.
- `TownDefense` (`city.defense`) is bandits on the roads (monthly roll by link and the stores' temptation, a warning, a raid), the watch posts, and the militia call-up; a called-up family's workplace makes nothing (`City.apply_building_output_modifiers`).
- `SettlementStage` (`city.settlement`) watches the town and records its stage (camp, hamlet, village; titles never drop back) and chronicle of firsts; it changes nothing in the town.
- `scripts/ui/town_status_bar.gd` (`TownStatusBar`) is the town's status chips in the top bar (people, food in days, free homes, labor, and alerts while they matter), drawn by `main.gd`'s `draw_town_status_chips` with detail on hover; the sidebar no longer repeats them.
